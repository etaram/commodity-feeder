# Sinder targets (owner-approved 13 Nov 2026, 15:24 Israel)

The owner asked for realistic, professional targets. 1,000,000 OPEN opportunities and 1,000,000 REFINED suppliers
stay the long-term direction; these are the binding milestones. Only quality-passing records count.

Definitions (unchanged): OPEN = a real, validated future deadline from the source, cancelled/removed notices out;
REFINED = passes the current quality gate (identity with ISO country, own site read with high name match, role, real
products, official contact, checked within 180 days). Counts come from the live index (morning-check) and the gate
run, with the run id.

Where the volume comes from (estimates, to be replaced by measurements):
- OPEN: TED full sweep (+10k), Japan KKJ (+1.5-5k), US state/local BidNet (+20k), Bonfire/bidsandtenders/OpenGov
  (+5-15k), India GeM via relay (+40k), Korea KONEPS (+15-30k), private procurement pages (unknown, measuring), other
  national portals from the Sources pipeline.
- REFINED: enrich the remaining ~200k award winners, national company registries (official bulk files), better role
  and official-contact evidence (the two main blockers), procurement-page companies.

Who owns what (daily):
- Claude: implementation, server, integration, daily measurement, owner reports.
- ChatGPT: evidence and rights review within 4 hours of each delivery; access team (blocked sources).
- Gemini: 10 new official sources per day, each with a real dated sample and an open count; hub/agency lists.
- Grok: infrastructure-company sources with exact endpoints and terms, 10 per day.
A missed delivery is reported in the sheet with the reason the same day.

Europe and India keep growing, but new effort goes to the regions above first.
