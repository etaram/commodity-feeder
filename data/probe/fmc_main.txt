<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="about" class="wp-block-heading">About</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ocean Transportation Intermediaries (OTIs) are either Ocean Freight Forwarders or Non-Vessel-Operating Common Carriers and are regulated by the FMC pursuant to the&nbsp;Shipping Act of 1984.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An&nbsp;<strong>Ocean Freight Forwarder (OFF)</strong>&nbsp;is an individual or company located in the U.S. which:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>arranges cargo movement to an international destination</li>



<li>dispatches shipments from the United States via common carriers and books or otherwise arranges space for those shipments on behalf of shippers</li>



<li>prepares and processes the documentation and performs related activities pertaining to those shipments</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Prior to Offering Services in the U.S. Trades, OFFs:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-515#515.3">required to obtain a license from the FMC</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 515.3)</li>



<li>are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-515#subpart-C">required to submit proof of financial responsibility for payment of claims</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR Part 515 Subpart&nbsp;C) arising from transportation-related activities</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A&nbsp;<strong>Non-Vessel-Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC)</strong>&nbsp;is:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>a common carrier that holds itself out to the public to provide ocean transportation, issues its own house bill of lading or equivalent document, and does not operate the vessels by which ocean transportation is provided</li>



<li>a shipper in its relationship with the vessel-operating common carrier involved in the movement of cargo</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Prior to Offering Services in the U.S. Trades, NVOCCs:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-515#515.3">required to obtain a license from the FMC</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 515.3)&nbsp;(if a U.S.-based company)</li>



<li>are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-515#subpart-C">required to submit proof of financial responsibility for payment of claims</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR Part 515 Subpart&nbsp;C)&nbsp;(arising from its transportation-related activities</li>



<li>are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-520#520.3">required to publish a tariff</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 520.3)</li>
</ul>



<div style="height:14px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="applying-for-an-oti-license" class="wp-block-heading">Applying For an OTI License</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">U.S.-based companies or sole proprietors operating as OFFs or NVOCCs are required&nbsp;<a href="https://www.fmc.gov/licensing-and-certification/apply-for-a-license-or-request-a-foreign-registration/">to obtain a license from the FMC</a>. Non-U.S.-based NVOCCs may obtain an FMC-issued license if they have a qualifying US presence.</p>



<div style="height:14px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="publication-of-tariffs" class="wp-block-heading">Publication Of Tariffs</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All NVOCCs operating in the U.S. trades are&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-520#520.3">required to publish a tariff</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 520.3). &nbsp;Tariffs must be open for public inspection&nbsp;and show all rates, charges, classifications, rules, and practices between all points or ports on their service routes.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tariffs must be published:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Prior to a license being issued, and prior to commencement of services, a NVOCC must provide the Commission its organization name, home office address, name and telephone number of firm’s representative, the location of its tariff(s), and the publisher used to maintain its tariff(s), and submit this information using&nbsp;<a href="https://www2.fmc.gov/Form1">Form FMC-1</a>. (<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/part-520/section-520.3#p-520.3(d)">46 CFR § 520.3(d)</a>)</li>



<li>If there is any change to the information on Form FMC-1, it must be submitted to the Commission within 30 days.</li>



<li>A non-U.S.-based NVOCC must list in its tariff an agent for service of process in the United States. ((<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/part-520/section-520.11#p-520.11(b)">46 CFR 520.11(b)</a>)</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Commission publishes the location of all&nbsp;<a href="https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC1Users/scripts/ExtReports.asp?tariffClass=oti">NVOCC tariffs online</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Please see the below relevant industry advisories:</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/articles/commission-advisory-regarding-red-sea-surcharges/">Commission Advisory Regarding Red Sea Surcharges</a><br>Posted: January 12, 2024</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/articles/industry-advisory-requiring-no-cost-access-to-tariff-publication-systems/">Industry Advisory – Requiring No Cost Access to Tariff Publication Systems</a><br>Posted: January 4, 2024</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/articles/industry-advisory-names-of-non-compliant-nvoccs-posted-to-fmc-website/">Industry Advisory – Names of Non-Compliant NVOCCs Posted to FMC Website</a><br>Posted: August 31, 2023</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/articles/industry-advisory-requirements-for-doing-business-with-nvoccs-and-offs-2/">Industry Advisory — Requirements for Doing Business with NVOCCs and OFFs</a><br>Posted: July 13, 2023</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/articles/industry-advisory-requirement-to-maintain-accurate-information-on-fmc-form-fmc-1/">Industry Advisory—Requirement to Maintain Accurate Information on FMC Form FMC-1</a><br>Posted: January 10, 2023</p>

</div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="service-contracts" class="wp-block-heading">Service Contracts</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.fmc.gov/complaints-and-assistance/how-to-file-service-contracts/">A NVOCC may enter into a service contract</a>&nbsp;for transportation services with a vessel-operating common carrier as a shipper signatory. The shipper party (NVOCC) must certify on the signature page its shipper status.</p>



<div style="height:14px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="nvocc-service-arrangements-nsas" class="wp-block-heading">NVOCC Service Arrangements (NSAS)</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">NVOCCs that are in compliance with the requirements of&nbsp;<a href="https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title46/subtitle4/partA/chapter409&amp;edition=prelim">the Shipping Act (46 USC 40901-40904)</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-515">the Commission’s licensing and financial responsibility requirements</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 515) may enter into an NSA with one or more NSA Shippers subject to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-531">the Commission’s rules governing NVOCC Service Arrangements</a>&nbsp;(46 CFR § 531).</p>



<div style="height:14px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 id="nvocc-negotiated-rate-arrangements-nras" class="wp-block-heading">NVOCC Negotiated Rate Arrangements (NRAS)</h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">NVOCCs may enter into negotiated rate arrangements when provided exception from certain tariff rate publication requirements. The FMC procedures and requirements to qualify for and use the exemption are found in&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-532">Part 532 of the Commission’s Regulations</a>.</p>



<div style="height:14px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>
