Ensuring Transparency in Public Procurement!

Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) is an autonomous regulatory body established under the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority Ordinance, 2002. Operating under the Cabinet Division, Government of Pakistan, PPRA is entrusted with the responsibility to ensure transparency, accountability, and efficiency in public procurement processes across all federal government organizations.

Procurement Services & Public Information!

This section provides access to procurement-related services, guidelines, notifications, and public information to support stakeholders in understanding and participating in the public procurement process.

Rules & Regulations

PPRA provides the legal and regulatory framework for public procurement in Pakistan, ensuring transparency, fairness, and value for money.

SBD / SPD Templates

PPRA issues SBDs and SPDs to standardize procurement procedures and ensure transparency, fairness, and compliance.

Open Contracting Data Standard(OCDS)

PPRA adopts the Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) to enhance transparency and public access to procurement information.

Policy Guidelines

PPRA issues policy guidelines to support uniform interpretation and effective implementation of public procurement rules.

EPADS Registered Suppliers

EPADS provides a centralized registry of verified suppliers and bidders eligible to participate in public procurements.
