DTN designates a significant move as anything 5% or more.</p>  <p class="news_content">Three fertilizers had a considerable price increase. UAN32 was 6% more expensive looking back a month. The nitrogen fertilizer had an average price of $486/ton.</p>  <p class="news_content">Both anhydrous and UAN28 were 5% higher compared to the prior month. Anhydrous had an average price of $987/ton, while UAN28 was $441/ton.</p>  <p class="news_content">The remaining four nutrients' prices were just slightly more expensive. DAP had an average price of $937/ton, MAP $981/ton, potash $503/ton and urea $674/ton.</p>

S&amp;P Global Ratings said the disruption to fertilizer supply chains is evident as the Strait of Hormuz conflict continues.</p>  <p class="news_content">Higher nitrogen and phosphorus nutrient prices relative to agricultural commodity prices have weakened fertilizer affordability globally.
