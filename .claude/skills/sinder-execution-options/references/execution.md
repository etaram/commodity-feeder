# Execution option contract
Each option: provider/legal entity, role, source/date, applicable origin/destination/product, scope, conditions, expiration, price status, missing inputs and next verification action.

Logistics: volume/mass/dimensions, packaging, bulk/container, dangerous-goods requirements if applicable, origin/destination, mode, Incoterm and schedule. Distinguish capability from available space or binding rate.

Finance: applicant, borrower/beneficiary roles, country, instrument, amount, tenor, repayment source, eligibility evidence and conditions. Directory region/sector overlap cannot establish eligibility. No automatic approvals.

Partners: required role and qualifications, identity, geographic coverage, private relationship scope and engagement status. Do not assume relationships transfer between clients/workspaces.

Provider recommendations can remain conditional research. Do not invent freight, duties, coverage, bank terms, stock or acceptance.
