# Assess transaction execution
Read references/operating-contract.md and references/execution.md.

1. Collect origin, destination, product/cargo, quantity, shipment characteristics, delivery term, schedule and contracting parties.
2. Retrieve route-specific logistics capability. Regional presence alone is insufficient. Separate freight forwarder, carrier, customs, inspection and insurance roles.
3. For finance collect applicant/borrower, jurisdiction, amount, tenor, cash cycle, instruments, eligibility evidence and outstanding conditions.
4. For partners verify identity, country, role, actual capability and engagement status. Private partner relationships stay workspace-scoped.
5. Label each option: directory candidate, evidence-supported capability, conditional eligibility, indicative quote, firm offer. Do not promote based on a region/sector tag alone.
6. Compare comparable scopes and include expiration, dependencies and evidence. Refer supplied numbers to the economics workflow.
7. List the cheapest next information request: freight dimensions, formal offer, bank eligibility check, partner confirmation, inspection scope.
8. Update the existing deal dossier and next actions without booking, binding coverage, opening financial products or moving funds.

Verify changing rules/requirements from current authoritative sources. Treat unresolved legal/compliance questions as conditions for qualified review. Do not make unsupported categorical regulatory claims.
