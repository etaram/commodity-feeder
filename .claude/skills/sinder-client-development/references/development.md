# Client and campaign contract
Preserve client_id, workspace_id, permissions and private claim scope. Parse native document text first, preserving slide/page references; review embedded visuals only when material.

Dossier: identity, products, mandatory technical boundaries, use cases, differentiators, references, markets, delivery/service footprint, approved claims and exclusions. Mark client statements until corroborated.

Prospect levels: structural fit, evidenced need, current buying signal. End buyer, contractor, integrator and channel partner require different propositions. Match RO capacity and feed/output conditions; do not use industry keywords alone.

Draft pilot plans first. Authorization must identify sender, audience, channels, templates/approved claims, attachments, cadence, limits and stop conditions. Recheck before every message. Research instructions are not send permission. Do not enroll real contacts or create accounts as part of a demonstration.

Optional Apollo/Clay enrichment may cost credits and may create saved records depending on the actual tool. Inspect schemas and cost/side effects before use; prefer public/cached facts first. These are connectors, not domain skills or sources of product-capability proof.

LinkedIn support may be a manual task queue. Use supported permitted access; no fake identities or evasion. Log actual responses and pass qualified needs to the existing deal builder.
