# Intelligence contract
Separate event_date, publication_date, effective_date and collected_at. Keep project country distinct from publisher and buyer headquarters. Attribute forecasts and their assumptions. Index project → package → tender → award → contractor relationships without requiring graph infrastructure.

Use fact → interpretation → counter-hypothesis → commercial mechanism → validation action. Avoid an obligatory commercial conclusion. Confidence is heuristic unless calibrated.

For price data keep specification, market, unit, currency, timestamp, term and price type. Commodity attention indices and Google Trends are discovery signals only. Do not compare CIF with FOB or futures with a physical supplier offer without a documented normalization.

Track what changed and reprocess only changes. A mirrored press release is one evidence family. A development bank approval is financing for a project, not necessarily a purchase order or eligible credit for Sinder.
