# Analyze events and markets
Read references/operating-contract.md and references/intelligence.md.

1. Retrieve current permitted primary evidence and retain publication, event, effective and collection dates separately.
2. Normalize project/buyer/publisher/supplier/affected countries independently. Deduplicate mirrored articles and repeated announcements.
3. Identify the actual change: planning, funding, permit, EPC selection, tender, award, package, disruption or price movement. Link to the project timeline.
4. Explain verified fact, inference, alternative explanation and what would falsify the inference.
5. Compare supply, demand, inventory, trade and logistics signals where available. A production decline alone does not prove an import gap; forecasts remain conditional.
6. For prices normalize product, grade, origin, destination, unit, currency, Incoterm, timestamp and spot/futures/index/offer status before arithmetic.
7. Treat Google Trends as relative attention with methodology, not absolute demand or buying intent.
8. Return world intelligence even when no trade exists. Where justified, add a research hypothesis, likely procurement package, evidenced actors, timing range and next validation action.
9. Trigger dossier re-evaluation only for affected requirements and evidence. Avoid AI calls for unchanged feeds.

Do not convert a press mention into confirmed budget, an announced plant into current buying intent, or a benchmark spread into executable arbitrage.
