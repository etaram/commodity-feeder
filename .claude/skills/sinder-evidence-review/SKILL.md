# Review evidence and readiness
Read references/operating-contract.md and references/review.md.

1. Separate fact accuracy, exact requirement fit, strategic relevance, route feasibility and readiness.
2. Trace decision-critical claims to original evidence. Check attribution, date, document context, amendments and independence. A model label is not an independent source.
3. Challenge the strongest commercial conclusion with counter-evidence and an alternative explanation. Find the cheapest test that could settle the uncertainty.
4. Check entity versus group, product sold versus shipment context, operator versus equipment seller, benchmark versus offer, potential demand versus active buying, and directory coverage versus deal eligibility.
5. Inspect numeric inputs and definitions. Do not let NaN, unknown goods share, impossible phone numbers or a broad keyword raise scores.
6. Review privacy, permitted retrieval and action scope through actual backend policies. Prompt instructions are not access control.
7. Return supported claims, disputed/rejected claims, research gaps, material blockers and allowed next stage. Keep useful uncertain candidates in research.
8. Distinguish unavailable metrics from zero, and processed coverage from verified/enriched completeness. Report denominator and measurement method.
9. Evaluate matching on a held-out adjudicated sample; show model-only review as model-only, not human precision or proven win probability.

Do not be pedantic about harmless formatting or demand perfect data before useful research. Focus on errors that change counterpart choice, route, economics or the next action.
