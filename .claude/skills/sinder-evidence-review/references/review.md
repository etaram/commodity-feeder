# Review rubric
Material defects change counterpart selection, commercial route, cost, timing, permission or the next action. Prioritize those over harmless presentation imperfections.

Check:
- entity-level attribution and product-specific evidence;
- original source, dates, independent evidence families;
- specification compatibility and contradictory details;
- route conditions and assumptions;
- quote scope, price basis, expiry and comparable units;
- contact provenance and structural plausibility;
- stale conclusions and version changes;
- privacy/action authorization enforced in the host;
- metrics scope, denominator, missing data and model-only labels.

Return a claim table with verdict, evidence, conflict/gap and next step. Recommend research for unresolved promising candidates. Do not block useful investigation because every field is not complete.

Regression case: an impossible-looking phone added to a company must not increase readiness merely because the field is non-empty. A source-backed contact form can be useful even with no email. A quote alone does not prove financial capacity or actual delivery ability.
