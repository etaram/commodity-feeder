# Route assessment contract
Return one row per route: direct, agency, local partner/distributor, EPC/sub-supplier. Include verdict (supported/conditional/blocked/unknown), source clause/page, assumptions, missing documents, owner and next action.

Use amendments and timezone-aware deadlines. Check product origin separately from bidder registration. Distinguish planned, open, closed, awarded and exemption notices. A main award can generate contractor supply research, not an open bid.

Local-partner presence in a profile must have an affirmative, scoped relationship value; dictionary-key existence is insufficient. A candidate must not count as engagement. Do not treat “World Bank” or “international” elsewhere in a document as overriding an explicit domestic clause.

Extract exact licence/certification/authorization requirements. Do not produce blanket bans based on words such as blood or plasma, and do not give unsupported legal advice. Refer material unresolved interpretation for qualified review. Prepare checklists; do not sign or submit.
