# Assess tender entry routes
Read references/operating-contract.md and references/routes.md.
Use the relevant existing PDF/DOCX/XLSX skill to extract actual documents. Preserve page/section references and amendments.

1. Confirm notice identity, buyer, stage, lots, deadline/timezone and latest amendment. Treat expired, planned, awarded and open notices differently.
2. Extract goods share, line items, quantities, technical standards, qualification, origin, bidder registration, security/bonds, payment and delivery requirements.
3. Resolve explicit positive and negative eligibility statements before broad source/IFI signals. An IFI mention cannot erase a domestic restriction.
4. Compare direct foreign bidder, local distributor/partner, agency and subcontract supply. Distinguish bidder eligibility from permitted product origin.
5. Track partner states: required, candidate, identity checked, capability reviewed, engagement confirmed. Never assume a partner circumvents every restriction.
6. Identify contractor/EPC supply routes even after main award where appropriate; do not call an awarded tender open.
7. Return a route matrix: evidence, conditions, blockers, missing documents, owner and next action. Label interpretations and unresolved legal questions.
8. Create a submission checklist and research tasks, without submitting a bid or accepting terms.

Do not infer that pharmaceuticals are always impossible or that a large infrastructure contract requires Sinder to bid as principal. Evaluate the exact product, lot and role.
