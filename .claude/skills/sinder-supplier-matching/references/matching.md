# Matching contract
Use normalized product-family assertions plus mandatory attributes. Example requirement: product_family=maize, color=white, form=whole grain. The word corn establishes neither color nor form. Company names and group brand names do not count as product evidence.

Allow producer, merchant and distributor routes when capability supports the job. Distinguish equipment producer, operator, installer, service provider and transport context. Supplier score and requirement-specific fit are different.

Require sourced assertions; a description of a portfolio is evidence of a company claim, not a capacity guarantee. Prefer exact direct evidence, then candidate research. Conflicting attributes require review. A company may make several products: scope the evidence to the relevant product instead of rejecting its whole portfolio.

The Python guard intentionally implements only a small conservative subset and the supplied English regression cases. Preserve multilingual originals and use evaluated extraction/ontology for broader production use. Do not use it as a universal semantic search engine.

Evaluate reviewed precision and independently curated recall by family/role. Separate model-only checks from human adjudication. Return fewer supported matches honestly and keep useful plausible candidates visible.
